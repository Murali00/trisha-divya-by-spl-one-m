Your Special One

Every girl needs one person who gets the full, unedited version — the one who hears it first, understands it fastest, and never needs the backstory.

For you, that's me. The one who sat up late picking photos, writing every line on these pages, and smiling the whole time doing it. Not a title — just a place in your life I'm really proud to have.

Us, in short

Not every special person needs a label.

Some people just become part of your everyday — and that's more than enough.

We talk almost every day. About important things, and much more often about completely unimportant things. We share almost everything, we joke around, we support each other, and somewhere along the way we learned each other's tiny habits by heart.

Through good days, bad days and “I'm bored, talk to me” days, I've got her back as her special one. No titles, no big words — just a friendship that's comfortable enough to be completely normal, and special enough to make a website about.
