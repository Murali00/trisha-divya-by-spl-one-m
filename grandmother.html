Chapter 05 · a quiet page

For her Paati

Some people leave this world, but they never leave our hearts.

Her bond with her grandmother

Among all the people in her childhood, there was one she was especially close to — her maternal grandmother. Her Paati.

She was still a little girl in 6th standard when her Paati passed away. Young enough that the world didn't fully make sense yet; old enough to know she had lost someone who mattered to her deeply.

That kind of bond doesn't really end. It just changes shape — into a memory, a feeling, a quiet place inside that she can always go back to.

We don't need to tell stories here. Some love is too personal to be written down by anyone else. This page simply says: she is remembered.

Love like that doesn't fade with the years. It grows roots, and it stays.

A little lamp, for her

For the one who is always remembered, and always loved.

If there's a softness in her today, maybe a little of it was borrowed from her Paati — and kept safe all these years.
