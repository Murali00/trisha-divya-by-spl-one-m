Before you scroll

A little note, before the pages.

(yes, I actually made this.)

Some people arrive in your life with a lot of noise. You didn't. You just quietly became part of the everyday — the random messages, the long talks, the “listen, one small thing happened” that is never, ever small.

Not every special person needs a label. Some people simply become the place your day goes when something happens — good, bad, or completely silly.

So this isn't a grand gesture. It's a collection: of photos, of habits, of the people who shaped you, and of all the little things that make you you. Happy almost-23.
