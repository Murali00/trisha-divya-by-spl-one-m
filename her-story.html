23 years · in progress
The beginning · 19 December

A girl with two names

It started on a December day, in a home with Appa Ravichandran and Amma Rekha — and her brother Madhan, built-in partner for sharing everything (and arguing over everything). Trisha to some, Divya to others. Either way, the world got someone special.

Childhood

Her Paati's girl

Some of her deepest bonds were made early — none more than the one with her maternal grandmother. She lost her Paati while she was in 6th standard, and learned, far too young, that some people stay in your heart long after they're gone.

A quiet page for her Paati
School days

Growing up, one class at a time

Uniforms, homework, exams, friendships — and slowly becoming the person she is now. The real details are hers to tell. She tells them best anyway, usually with a lot of side stories.

The classroom era

Desks, deadlines & a perfect dupatta

Lectures, notes, assignments and submissions — and still managing to look this composed inside a classroom. That alone deserves a separate degree.

Master's degree

MBA in Human Resources

Out of every subject in the world, she chose to study people — how they work, what they need, and how to bring out their best. MBA in HR: completed. Certificate earned, chapter closed with a smile.

Right now

Choosing the right beginning

Not in an office yet. Not in a hurry either. She's figuring out what comes next — looking for the right door, not just the first one that opens. And honestly? That's a very HR thing to do.

Coming soon

The chapter she hasn't written yet

A career, new routines, new people, a better version of the same lovely girl. Status: loading — at her speed, which is the only speed that counts here.
